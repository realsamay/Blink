# Voiceover, Audio & SFX Production Guide

This guide contains the exact spoken script, voice direction, and sound design markers to create an authentic Apple-style launch video soundtrack.

---

## 🎙️ 1. Voiceover Script & Direction (For ElevenLabs / Murf / Speechify)

### Voice Style & Personality
- **Tone**: Calm, thoughtful, visionary, quietly confident, authoritative.
- **Pacing**: Measured and deliberate, with intentional pauses for visual emphasis (classic Apple keynote / design documentary style, reminiscent of Jony Ive or Craig Federighi).
- **ElevenLabs Recommended Voice**: **"Adam"**, **"Antony"**, **"George"**, or **"Callum"** (British / Neutral Atlantic accent, crisp articulation, warm resonance).
- **Settings**:
  - Stability: 65% - 70%
  - Clarity / Similarity: 80% - 85%
  - Style Exaggeration: 10% - 15%

## 🎵 2. Soundtrack & Music Direction (For Suno / Udio / Epidemic Sound)

### Music Style
- **Genre**: Minimalist Cinematic Electronic / Ambient Keynote Indie Tech.
- **Reference Tracks**:
  - Apple Event intro music (The Blaze, Tycho, Jon Hopkins, Max Richter).
  - Clean analog synth arpeggios, warm sub-bass pulse, delicate crisp percussive claps, crescendo swell at 0:11 (the unlock moment), settling into a confident upbeat synth groove.
- **AI Music Generation Prompt (Suno / Udio)**:
